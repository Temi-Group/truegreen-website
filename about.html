Sustainable solutions. Lasting impact.

Lifetime Truegreen Developments is a South African infrastructure company building cleaner, more resilient homes, estates and businesses through solar, battery backup and water purification.

Who We Are

Energy and water, engineered for the long term

We design, install, own and operate sustainable infrastructure — bringing generation, storage, purification and intelligent control together on professionally managed platforms.

From a single home to entire residential estates, our mission is simple: deliver clean energy and pure water that are reliable, affordable and built to last a lifetime.

Our Mission

To make clean energy and pure water accessible, reliable and affordable — and to deliver it intelligently, at every scale.

Our Approach

Turnkey and certified. We handle assessment, design, supply, installation, commissioning and ongoing support, so our clients carry none of the complexity.

Our Promise

Quality that lasts. Every system is built to recognised standards, certified for safe operation and backed by warranties and after-sales service.
