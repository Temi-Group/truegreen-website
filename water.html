5-Stage Purification Process

From raw water to pure output

1

Multimedia Pre-Filter

Sand, anthracite & gravel remove suspended solids down to 20 microns.

2

Activated Carbon

Removes chlorine, chloramines, organics, taste, odour and colour.

3

Water Softening

Ion-exchange resin removes calcium & magnesium hardness.

4

Reverse Osmosis

0.0001μm membranes reject 97–99% of dissolved salts & metals.

5

UV + Antibacterial

254nm UV and post-treatment eliminate 99.99% of microorganisms.
