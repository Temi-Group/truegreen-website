How It Works

From sunlight to stored power

1

Solar Generation

Monocrystalline panels convert sunlight into DC electricity all day.

2

Hybrid Inverter

Converts DC to clean AC and routes power between loads, battery and grid.

3

Lithium Storage

Surplus energy is stored in LiFePO₄ batteries for night and outages.

4

Smart Management

App prioritises solar first, then battery, then grid — automatically.

5

Seamless Backup

Automatic switchover keeps essential circuits running through load-shedding.
